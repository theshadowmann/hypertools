Hyperliquid trading terminal: live chart, order book, and market/limit orders from a connected wallet. Account overview for balances, positions, and staking.

Not financial advice. HyperTools does not recommend any trade, leverage, or market. Nothing on this page is an offer, solicitation, or investment recommendation.

Not affiliated with Hyperliquid. This is an independent site. It is not built, endorsed, or operated by Hyperliquid or its affiliates.

Live data, still estimates. Account figures are fetched from Hyperliquid’s public Info API. They can be stale, incomplete, or disagree with the official UI. Liquidation prices are reported by the venue; a JSON null is shown as an em dash, not zero. If a number here disagrees with Hyperliquid, trust Hyperliquid.

Wallet trading. Connect a wallet to place and cancel Hyperliquid perps orders. Connecting requests eth_requestAccounts. Enable trading signs official Hyperliquid EIP-712 messages (approveBuilderFee, approveAgent). Orders are L1 actions signed by the agent key in this tab’s memory — wiped on Disconnect, never written to localStorage, never sent to a server. Paste an address to load an account.

Do your own research. Trading perpetual futures can result in total loss of capital. Only risk what you can afford to lose.
